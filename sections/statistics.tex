\chapter{Statistical Notions}

This chapter is a placeholder for the statistics side of the atlas. The intended focus is not ``statistical learning theory'' in the broad sense, but specific statistical notions that are often discussed as if they were notions of learning.

Planned entries include:

\begin{itemize}
\item consistency
\item Bayes consistency
\item unbiasedness
\item admissibility
\item asymptotic normality
\item minimax optimality
\end{itemize}

The key design rule will be the same as in the PAC chapter: every notion should be entered with a precise definition, a statement of what resources or asymptotics it controls, and a list of known implications or non-implications to neighboring notions.
