# Oracle-semantics compatibility matrix to close Route D

ID: idea_01

## Summary
Build a theorem-accurate compatibility matrix for each candidate booster family (KMV08, Feldman 2008/2012 variants, potential-based boosting): exact source theorem ID, weak-oracle contract, required output format (hypothesis/edge predictor/confidence), conversion of err≤OPT+β to its (α,γ) form, final excess form (OPT+ε vs OPT+α+ε), and precise polynomial dependence on ε, β, and failure probability.

## Why Promising
This directly resolves the highest-priority blocker and prevents false-positive claims; once complete, any subsequent positive/negative route is either immediately constrained or ruled out.

## Risks
Most of this work is bibliographic and can stall if canonical theorems in KMV08/Feldman/potential frameworks already assume richer interface than fixed 0/1 excess-risk; some papers may only be conditionally equivalent under distribution-specific or realizability variants.
