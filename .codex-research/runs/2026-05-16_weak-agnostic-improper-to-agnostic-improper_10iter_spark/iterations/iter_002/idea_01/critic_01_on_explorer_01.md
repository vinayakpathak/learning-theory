## Summary
The explorer report is directionally sound and honest about uncertainty, but several claims are not yet locked down.

For a critic pass, the most important outcome is:  
- No concrete theorem is yet established that turns the fixed-slack oracle  
`err ≤ OPT + β` (constant β, no tunable α/γ) into `opt + ε` under the project’s efficiency model.
- The strongest blocker is correctly identified: known agnostic boosting theorems typically preserve an additive floor tied to the weak model, not eliminate it.
- However, the report contains at least one formal-derivation gap (translation from fixed-slack oracle to known `(α,γ)`-weak hypotheses), one citation weakness (non-primary source reliance), and a couple of overstrong wording claims that need stronger qualifiers.

   - Classification: unsupported citation.

## Counterexamples Or Stress Tests
1. [Great candidate stress test]  
   Construct a low-`OPT` distribution where the fixed oracle can return correlated noise-like hypotheses with excess about β but whose adaptive reweighting leads `OPT` near 1/2 on filtered subsets.  
   - Purpose: directly tests the alleged “filter instability” obstruction.

2. [Mass-decay stress test]  
   Use sparse/highly concentrated residual regions and adversarially chosen reweighting so acceptance mass shrinks exponentially; check whether any supposed wrapper preserves polynomial sample complexity.  
   - Purpose: falsifies naive residual-forcing unless mass lower bounds are explicit.

3. [Non-elimination test for additive floor]  
   For concept classes with `OPT=0` on base distribution, attempt to instantiate the oracle outputs so any feasible adaptive wrapper still cannot beat `β + Ω(1)` excess under distribution shifts generated by the wrapper.  
   - Purpose: probes whether the “offset-floor” is genuinely fundamental in this model or artifact of current proof style.

4. [Oracle-format stress test]  
   Convert a source theorem with label-to-label weak calls into this exact binary-only fixed-slack format and check whether required stronger outputs (confidence/margins/pseudo-labels) are implicitly used.  
   - Purpose: catches semantic gap errors.
