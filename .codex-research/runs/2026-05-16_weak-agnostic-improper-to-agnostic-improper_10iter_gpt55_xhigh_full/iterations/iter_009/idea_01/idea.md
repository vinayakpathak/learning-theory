# Instantiate Approximation-Gap Separation

ID: idea_01

## Summary
Pick one concrete succinct-domain optimization class and test the full approximation-gap template end-to-end: define the query domain, keep the sign/RHS as the label, prove or cite a distribution-free signed/weighted constant-approximation algorithm giving fixed slack, and formulate strong-side RCN hardness at eta >= gamma against arbitrary evaluable improper predictors.

## Why Promising
The solver attempt isolates the cleanest conditional negative template. A single fully checked candidate would turn the current vague route into either a serious conditional separation or a precise no-go checklist.

## Risks
The candidate may only have proper or refutation hardness, not improper prediction hardness. The weak approximator may fail for arbitrary signed/weighted distribution-free instances, or the domain may become table-learnable/public-label trivial.

## Suggested Lenses
- conditional separation
- candidate audit
- hardness sanity check
