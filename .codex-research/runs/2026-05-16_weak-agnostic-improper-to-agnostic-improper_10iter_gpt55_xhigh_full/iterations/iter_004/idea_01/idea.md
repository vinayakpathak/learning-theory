# Clean-Transcript Dichotomy

ID: idea_01

## Summary
Turn the solver's clean-transcript menu into a sharp dichotomy: either prove a black-box wrapper using the fixed weak source must, in some formal model, create a complete final classifier from total hidden-clean witness length K with inverse-polynomial discovery mass, or identify a non-transcript mechanism that escapes the p^{-K} bottleneck. Make the quantifiers exact: public data U, comparator c, p=Pr[Y=c(X)], witness length K, success q, advice/search cost, and validation independence.

## Why Promising
This targets the current central bottleneck directly. A necessity theorem would explain why known boosting proofs stall; a counterexample mechanism would point to a new positive route.

## Risks
This may only repackage the existing conditional lemma unless the explorer proves a new necessity statement or finds a real sufficient condition. It can also fail by excluding algorithms that use posterior-biased sampling, implicit optimization, or class structure without literal clean transcripts.

## Suggested Lenses
- proof strategy
- necessity characterization
- counterexample search
