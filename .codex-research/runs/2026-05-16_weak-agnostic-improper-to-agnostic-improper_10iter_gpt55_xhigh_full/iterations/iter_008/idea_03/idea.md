# Generated-Bag Hardness

ID: idea_03

## Summary
Test whether arbitrary Set-Cover-hard sparse-majority bags can be realized as bags generated by a semantically valid weak learner inside a DCHP-style procedure, perhaps using rare-slice junk or query-programmed outputs. The target is either a reduction showing generated bags can be worst-case, or a structural property showing why they cannot.

## Why Promising
Current sparse-majority ERM hardness is only for arbitrary bags. This direction attacks the main loophole in using that hardness against the DCHP positive route.

## Risks
The constructed weak learner may be too artificial, white-box removable, or fail to arise under the actual DCHP query distributions. It may remain only a black-box obstruction.

## Suggested Lenses
- reduction
- selector hardness
- black-box obstruction
