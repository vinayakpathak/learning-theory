# Amplify Parity Gap

ID: idea_02

## Summary
Investigate whether the inverse-polynomial parity near-separation can be converted into a fixed-constant gamma separation by direct products, block mixtures, noise padding, or meta-classes. Require that the resulting class has a distribution-free OPT+beta weak learner for a fixed beta<1/2, while strong improper learning still implies LPN-like prediction hardness.

## Why Promising
If possible, this would turn the solver's near miss into a genuine conditional counterexample. If impossible, the no-go would explain why fixed constant slack is qualitatively different.

## Risks
Most products make the weak side as hard as noisy parity at constant noise. Constants, validation, or table-learning escapes may destroy the intended separation.

## Suggested Lenses
- construction search
- counterexample search
- hardness amplification
