# Finite-Bag da Cunha Theorem

ID: idea_01

## Summary
Ask an explorer to restate the da Cunha-Hogsgaard-Paudice route using only the finite set of hypotheses actually generated by polynomially many weak calls, rather than global assumptions on Range(A). The target is a precise conditional theorem: if hidden-clean weak-call distributions can be generated or sampled without enumeration, then finite-list validation handles the final classifier and no global VC(H) assumption is needed.

## Why Promising
The solver attempt identifies controlled Range(A), dual VC, and final selector assumptions as the main gap. Some of these may be artifacts of proving uniform guarantees over an enormous output class, while the atlas learner only needs one validated finite final list.

## Risks
The finite-bag generalization may be circular because the generated bag depends on hidden clean samples that are being enumerated. It may collapse back to the same dual-VC or tuple-enumeration bottleneck already identified.
