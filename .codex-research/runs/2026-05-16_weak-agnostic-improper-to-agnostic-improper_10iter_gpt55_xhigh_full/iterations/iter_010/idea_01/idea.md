# Ghost-Sample Clean Transfer

ID: idea_01

## Summary
Try to reorganize the DCHP-style proof with two independent hidden-clean roles: one sample/transcript generates a bag, while an independent ghost clean sample certifies population performance on D_c^+. The explorer should state exact independence and mask-discovery requirements, then prove a transfer theorem or isolate the exponential step.

## Why Promising
This attacks the current middle bottleneck directly: clean-population transfer. If independence can be made real, finite-class or compression arguments become available.

## Risks
The second clean sample is also hidden, so the argument may collapse back to exponential mask enumeration or anti-hitting. Arbitrary weak learners can overfit the generation sample, and this still may not solve final sparse-majority selection.

## Suggested Lenses
- proof strategy
- anti-hitting obstruction
