# Stable Weak Learner Route

ID: idea_03

## Summary
Formulate a conditional positive theorem under natural regularity of the supplied weak learner: leave-one-out stability on low-OPT calls, small transcript compression, or distribution-dependent range quotients. Determine which assumptions suffice for CleanPopulationTransfer and which also give a polynomial final list.

## Why Promising
It turns the vague controlled-range hope into precise, checkable hypotheses and could yield a meaningful conditional atlas implication.

## Risks
The atlas source does not imply stability or compression; rare-slice junk refutes black-box range control. The assumptions may be nearly as strong as the desired conclusion, and the selector problem can remain separate.

## Suggested Lenses
- conditional theorem
- assumption audit
