# Succinct Signed-CSP Approximation Candidate

ID: idea_03

## Summary
Instantiate the approximation-gap template with one explicit succinct signed-CSP or refutation class. Define the descriptor domain so it is superpolynomial, keep the RHS/sign as the label, prove or cite a signed/weighted constant-factor approximator, and separately formulate RCN sample-access hardness against arbitrary improper predictors.

## Why Promising
The solver attempt gives a clean weak-side lemma: constant-factor agnostic approximation implies fixed-slack weak learning. A single candidate passing this checklist could yield a conditional separation.

## Risks
Known refutation or CSP algorithms may not give arbitrary signed distribution-free approximation. Strong-side hardness must be improper prediction hardness, not assignment recovery or refutation hardness.

## Suggested Lenses
- candidate construction
- literature audit
- hardness reduction
