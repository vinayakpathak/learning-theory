# Literature Map

## Agnostic Boosting

Relevance: known agnostic boosting frameworks are the closest positive route,
but many require a weak learner whose advantage is scale-sensitive on the
distributions produced by the booster, or have parameter dependence that may be
exponential in weak-sample/capacity quantities.

Iteration 1 status: no external sources checked. Need a parameter audit before
stating any theorem.

## Statistical Query Lower Bounds

Relevance: the "many tiny signals" and "large-correlation easy,
small-correlation hard" ideas resemble SQ/correlation-query lower bounds. The
right formal negative statement may be a fixed-tolerance SQ barrier rather than
a PAC oracle lower bound.

Iteration 1 status: conceptual connection only; no sourced result recorded.

## Hard-Core Sets And Boosting Duality

Relevance: any claim that adaptive reweighting cannot find high-correlation
residuals should be compared with hard-core set, minimax, and boosting-duality
arguments.

Iteration 1 status: literature check pending.
